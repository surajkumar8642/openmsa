# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community:

- respectful communication
- clear and constructive review comments
- reporting issues without personal attacks
- focusing on design, behavior, and security

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment or intimidation
- discrimination or hate speech
- doxxing or personal attacks
- publishing private data or secrets

## Enforcement

Reports are handled by project maintainers. For security-sensitive behavior or suspected abuse, review `SECURITY.md` and follow the escalation flow.
